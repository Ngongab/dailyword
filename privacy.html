Privacy policy

Daily Word – Study Bible · Last updated 8 October 2026

In short: what you write is yours. It stays on your phone unless you sign in to back it up, and nothing is sold or used for advertising.

Daily Word – Study Bible (“Daily Word”, “the app”) is made by Newtown Studio in Zambia. This page explains what the app keeps, where, and why. The same policy is in the app under Settings › Privacy.

On your phone

Your notes, sermons, highlights, verse notes, photos you add to notes, reading progress, reading position and settings are stored on your phone. They work without an account and without a connection.

If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional; it is needed only for backup and for Ask. Google tells us your name, your email address and an account number. We use them only to sign you in and to keep your backup yours.

Backup

When you are signed in, your notes, sermons, verse notes, highlights and reading progress (the chapters you mark read and the days you read) are copied to our database, hosted by Supabase in Ireland, so they are safe and can come back on a new phone. Only your account can read them. Photos are not backed up; they stay on the phone they were added on.

Ask

When you ask a question, the question and the verse it is about are sent to our server, which asks Anthropic’s Claude for the answer. When you ask for help in the editor, the words of that note are sent the same way.

  • Answers to the same question on the same verse are kept and shared, without saying who asked.
  • We count how many questions each account asks, to apply the monthly limit.
  • If you report an answer, we keep the report and your reason so we can review it.
  • Anthropic does not use these requests to train its models.

What we do not do

No advertising, no tracking across apps, no analytics, and we do not sell or share your information with anyone beyond the services named here: Google (sign-in), Supabase (backup and our server) and Anthropic (Ask).

Deleting your account

Settings › Delete account removes your account and everything backed up under it, straight away. What is on your phone stays until you remove the app. You can also ask us to delete it by email — see Delete your account.

Children

Daily Word is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here and in the app, with a new date at the top.
