Delete your account

Daily Word – Study Bible, by Newtown Studio

You can delete your Daily Word account and everything backed up under it at any time — in the app, or by email if you no longer have it.

In the app

  1. Open Daily Word and tap the round person button at the top right of HomeThat opens Settings.
  2. Scroll to About and tap Delete account
  3. Confirm with Delete accountYour account and its backup are deleted straight away, and you are signed out.

Without the app

Email us from the Google account you signed in with, and we will delete the account and its backup within 30 days — usually within a few days. We will reply to confirm.

Email a deletion request

What is deleted, and what is kept

Deleted

  • Your account (your name, email address and Google account number)
  • Backed-up notes, sermons, verse notes and highlights
  • Backed-up reading progress and streak days
  • Your count of Ask questions
  • Any answers you reported, and your reasons

Kept

  • Answers to questions, which are stored without saying who asked and are shared by everyone who asks the same question on the same verse
  • What is on your phone, until you remove the app — deleting the account does not reach into your phone

Nothing is kept for a waiting period: deletion is immediate in the app, and within 30 days by email. For more, see the privacy policy.
