What’s inside

Everything for reading, study and preaching. Nothing in the way.

A calm page that reads like a good book. Mark what speaks to you in one of five soft colours, and the words of Jesus are always set apart in blue.

Promise Command Growth Love Prayer

Five translations, offline

The World English Bible, Berean Standard, King James, Kiswahili and Chichewa — on your phone, with no data needed once installed.

Study every verse

Cross-references for every verse and translations side by side, a tap away from what you are reading. Study notes and book introductions are being written now.

Ask about any verse

Questions answered in plain words, always tied to the verse in front of you. When traditions read it differently, it says so fairly.

Notes that grow into sermons

Write on a verse in seconds. Start a sermon or lesson from a template, pull in what you have written before, and shape it into points.

Progress and streaks

Mark chapters as you finish them and watch the whole Bible fill in. One verse a day keeps your streak going — no pressure, no league tables.

Reading plans

Follow a plan through a book or a season, a few minutes a day. Missed a day? It simply waits for you.

For pastors and teachers

From a quiet note to the pulpit.

The editor is as simple as a to-do list: tap a line to write, press Enter for the next. Type a reference and the verse drops in.

  • Sermon and lesson templates, with a live estimate of how long it will take to preach
  • Outline view to move whole sections in a moment
  • Preach mode: large text, a running clock, and the screen stays on
  • Share as PDF, print, or send the outline on WhatsApp
